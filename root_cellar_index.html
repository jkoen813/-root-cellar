DISCOVER THE SODA

Not your ordinary root beer.

Root Cellar is developing a rich, sweet, botanically inspired root beer with bold sarsaparilla character and a smooth herbal finish. Small batches first. Get the flavor right. Let the rest follow.

The Original

A classic-style root beer with a full, creamy profile, deep botanical character and a finish that lingers.

Old Florida, not tropical

Our inspiration is inland Florida: dark water, cypress knees, Spanish moss, oak shade and orange groves.

The odd fellows

A top-hatted flamingo and an alligator are the unlikely ambassadors of Root Cellar. Their credentials remain under review.

Small-batch beginnings

We are starting with careful flavor trials, controlled carbonation and a tiny first label run before deciding what comes next.

The Founder's Run · 2026

The first label order is a limited run of 18 labels. These inaugural bottles mark the beginning of Root Cellar and are intended as the project's first collector-style edition.
